India · Jan 2014 to Oct 2026 · 154 months

The shape of money

Every month of Indian interest rates since 2014, drawn as one surface. The front edge is the RBI's policy rate. The back edge is the 10-year government bond. Scroll to watch the RBI move it.

Scroll ↓
2014

Short money cost more than long money

In February 2014 the 3-month rate stood at 9.09%, above the 10-year bond at 8.81%. Money markets were still tight after the RBI squeezed liquidity in 2013 to steady the rupee, so the front of the surface sits higher than the back.

2015 – 2019

The long glide down

The repo rate fell from 8.00% to 5.15% between January 2015 and October 2019, with two hikes in 2018 along the way. The front edge steps down while the 10-year drifts between 6.5% and 8.0%.

2020 – 2021

The floor

Pandemic cuts took the repo rate to 4.00% in May 2020. With the system flush with cash, the 3-month rate sank to 3.06% by November, below the policy rate itself. The 10-year held near 6%, which opened the widest gap on the surface: 3.18 points between 3-month and 10-year in April 2022.

2022 – 2024

250 basis points in nine months

From May 2022 to February 2023 the RBI raised the repo rate from 4.00% to 6.50%. The 3-month rate climbed from 3.93% to 6.88%, but the 10-year moved far less, from 7.11% to 7.36%. The front rose to meet the back, and by June 2024 the gap was just 0.19 points.

2025 – 2026

Cuts reached the front, not the back

The RBI cut 125 basis points between February and December 2025, to 5.25%. The 3-month rate followed it down to 5.29%. The 10-year bottomed at 6.27% in May 2025, then rose to 7.05% by April 2026. The surface steepened again.

October 2026

The first hike since 2023

On 7 October 2026 the RBI raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50%. Two days later the full curve ran from 5.45% at 3 months to 7.71% at 30 years. This last slice shows all nine maturities, which is the shape the whole surface would take with complete data.

Reading the surface

Only the bright lines are data. The sheet between them joins neighbouring maturities with straight lines so the shape is easy to see. Maturity is on a log scale, so overnight, 3 months and 10 years sit at readable distances.

Gaps

The monthly series end in July 2026, so August and September 2026 are blank. The October slice is a single day, 9 October, not a monthly average.
